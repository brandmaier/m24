# 📊🎲  **StatsQuest: The Inferential Statistics Adventure** 🎲📊  

**Welcome to *StatsQuest: The Inferential Statistics Adventure*!** This is a serious game designed to help students grasp the fundamentals of **sampling** and **inference statistics** in a fun and engaging way.  

## **About the Game**  
Set in a **classic top-down RPG-style world**, you play as a student exploring the **MSB Medical School Berlin**. Your mission? To gather data from fellow students, learn about different sampling techniques, and analyze your findings using **JASP**—a free and open-source statistical software.  

## **Key Features**  
✅ **Explore the university**: Navigate the actual MSB Medical School Berlin building in an immersive RPG world.  
✅ **Collect data**: Interact with NPCs to gather responses and build your dataset.  
✅ **Learn by doing**: Understand key statistical concepts like **random sampling, bias, variability, and inference**.  
✅ **Export your data**: Download your collected data and analyze it in **JASP** to draw inferential conclusions.  
✅ **Interactive challenges**: Complete quests and perform statistical data analysis to progress in the game.  

## **Why Play?**  
Traditional statistics courses can be dry and theoretical, but *StatsQuest* makes learning hands-on and engaging. Whether you're a beginner or need a refresher, this game will help you develop **practical statistical skills** while having fun!  

Join us in the world of *StatsQuest* and level up your statistics skills! 🚀
